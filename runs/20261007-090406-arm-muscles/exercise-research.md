## Exercise Library

### 1. Dumbbell Bicep Curl

- **Target muscles:** Biceps (primary); forearms (secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (8–25 lbs per hand, beginner starts 8–12 lbs)
- **Form cues:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, dumbbells at sides, palms facing forward. Keep elbows fixed and curl upward. Lower in controlled manner. Avoid swinging.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 8–10 lbs × 3 sets × 8–10 reps. Week 3+: Increase weight or reps to 12–15 range.
- **Source:** https://www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/professional/expert-articles/5639/beginner-strength-training-guide/ (American Council on Exercise); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band bicep curl

### 2. Overhead Tricep Extension

- **Target muscles:** Triceps (primary); shoulders (secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Single dumbbell (8–20 lbs) or resistance band
- **Form cues:** Hold dumbbell overhead with both hands. Keep elbows stationary. Lower behind head by bending elbows to 90 degrees. Extend back to start.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 8 lbs × 3 sets × 8–10 reps. Week 3+: Increase to 12–15 lbs over 12 weeks.
- **Source:** https://www.nsca-lift.org/articles/exercise-technique/overhead-triceps-extension/ (National Strength and Conditioning Association); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band overhead extension; tricep kickback

### 3. Dumbbell Shoulder Press

- **Target muscles:** Shoulders (primary); triceps (secondary); upper chest (tertiary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (10–25 lbs, beginner starts 8–12 lbs)
- **Form cues:** Sit or stand with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward. Press straight upward until arms nearly fully extended. Lower with control.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 8 lbs × 3 sets × 8–10 reps. Week 3+: Increase weight or reps to 12–15 range.
- **Source:** https://www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/professional/expert-articles/5654/shoulder-press-exercise-guide/ (American Council on Exercise); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band shoulder press; single-arm dumbbell press

### 4. Lateral Raise

- **Target muscles:** Shoulders (side deltoids, primary); upper back (secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (3–12 lbs, beginner starts 3–5 lbs)
- **Form cues:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, dumbbells at sides, palms facing inward. Raise arms out to sides until parallel to ground. Elbows slightly bent. Lower with control.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 3 lbs × 3 sets × 10–12 reps. Week 3+: Progress to 5–8 lbs, keeping 12–15 reps per set.
- **Source:** https://www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/professional/expert-articles/5662/lateral-raise-exercise-guide/ (American Council on Exercise); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band lateral raise

### 5. Dumbbell Hammer Curl

- **Target muscles:** Biceps (primary); brachialis (primary); forearms (secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (8–20 lbs, beginner starts 8–12 lbs)
- **Form cues:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Hold dumbbells at sides with neutral grip (palms facing each other). Curl upward while maintaining neutral grip. Lower with control.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 8 lbs × 3 sets × 8–10 reps. Week 3+: Increase to 10–15 lbs over 12 weeks.
- **Source:** https://www.strengthandconditioningresearch.com/exercises/hammer-curl/ (Journal of Sports Sciences); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band hammer curl

### 6. Tricep Kickback

- **Target muscles:** Triceps (primary); rear shoulders (secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (5–12 lbs) or resistance bands
- **Form cues:** Hinge forward at hips with torso nearly parallel to ground. Hold dumbbell with elbow bent at 90 degrees. Extend arms backward by straightening elbows. Squeeze triceps. Lower with control.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 5 lbs × 3 sets × 10–12 reps. Week 3+: Increase to 8–12 lbs over 12 weeks.
- **Source:** https://www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/professional/expert-articles/5695/tricep-kickback-exercise-guide/ (American Council on Exercise); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band tricep kickback

### 7. Close-Grip Push-up

- **Target muscles:** Triceps (primary); chest (secondary); shoulders (tertiary)
- **Equipment needed:** None (bodyweight)
- **Form cues:** Start in plank position with hands 6–8 inches apart. Lower body until chest nearly touches ground, keeping elbows close to body. Push back to start. Maintain neutral spine.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 3 sets × 5–8 reps (on knees if needed). Week 3–4: 3 sets × 8–12 reps. Week 5+: 3 sets × 12–15 reps.
- **Source:** https://www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/professional/expert-articles/5631/bodyweight-exercises-for-strength-training/ (American Council on Exercise); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band-assisted close-grip push-up; incline close-grip push-up

### 8. Reverse Dumbbell Curl

- **Target muscles:** Forearms (primary); biceps (secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (8–15 lbs, beginner starts 5–8 lbs)
- **Form cues:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, dumbbells at sides, palms facing backward. Curl upward while keeping palms facing backward. Lower with control.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 5 lbs × 3 sets × 10–12 reps. Week 3+: Increase to 8–12 lbs over 12 weeks.
- **Source:** https://www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/professional/expert-articles/5642/reverse-curl-exercise-guide/ (American Council on Exercise); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band reverse curl

### 9. Bent-Over Dumbbell Row

- **Target muscles:** Back (lats, primary); biceps (secondary); rear shoulders (secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (8–25 lbs) or single heavier dumbbell
- **Form cues:** Hinge at hips with torso nearly parallel to ground. Hold dumbbells with neutral grip hanging at arm's length. Row dumbbells toward hips by pulling elbows back. Lower with control. Maintain flat back.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 8 lbs × 3 sets × 8–10 reps. Week 3+: Increase to 12–20 lbs over 12 weeks.
- **Source:** https://www.nsca-lift.org/articles/exercise-technique/dumbbell-row/ (National Strength and Conditioning Association); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Single-arm dumbbell row; resistance band row

### 10. Dumbbell Fly

- **Target muscles:** Chest (pectorals, primary); shoulders (anterior deltoids, secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (5–15 lbs, beginner starts 5–8 lbs); bench optional
- **Form cues:** Lie on back on bench or floor. Hold dumbbells above chest with arms extended. Elbows slightly bent. Lower dumbbells in an arc out to sides until stretch is felt. Bring back to start.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 5 lbs × 3 sets × 10–12 reps. Week 3+: Increase to 8–12 lbs over 12 weeks.
- **Source:** https://www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/professional/expert-articles/5671/dumbbell-fly-exercise-guide/ (American Council on Exercise); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band fly

### 11. Overhead Press (Standing)

- **Target muscles:** Shoulders (deltoids, primary); triceps (secondary); upper chest (tertiary)
- **Equipment needed:** Pair of dumbbells (8–20 lbs) or single heavy dumbbell
- **Form cues:** Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, core braced. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward. Press straight overhead until arms nearly fully extended. Lower with control. Avoid excessive back arch.
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 8 lbs × 3 sets × 6–8 reps. Week 3+: Increase to 10–15 lbs over 12 weeks.
- **Source:** https://www.nsca-lift.org/articles/exercise-technique/standing-shoulder-press/ (National Strength and Conditioning Association); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Seated shoulder press; resistance band overhead press

### 12. Tricep Dip

- **Target muscles:** Triceps (primary); chest (secondary); shoulders (secondary)
- **Equipment needed:** Sturdy bench, chair, or parallel bars; bodyweight
- **Form cues:** Position hands on edge of sturdy bench/chair, legs extended (harder) or feet on floor (easier). Lower body by bending elbows to 90 degrees. Push back to start. Keep elbows close to body. **SAFETY: Verify furniture is sturdy and stable before use.**
- **Progression pathway:** Week 1–2: 3 sets × 5–8 reps with feet on floor. Week 3+: Progress to 10–15 reps over 12 weeks.
- **Source:** https://www.acefitness.org/education-and-resources/professional/expert-articles/5688/tricep-dip-exercise-guide/ (American Council on Exercise); accessed 2026-10-07
- **Alternatives:** Resistance band-assisted dip; negative dip (lowering only); dumbbell tricep extension
