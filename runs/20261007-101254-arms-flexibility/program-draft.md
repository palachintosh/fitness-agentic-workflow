# Your 1-Week Arm Flexibility Program

- **Owner:** program-designer
- **Status:** complete
- **Updated:** 2026-10-07
- **Focus:** Arm and shoulder flexibility for beginners targeting the ability to touch your back

## Program Overview

This 1-week program repeats week-to-week and takes approximately 25 minutes per day. Each session includes a brief warm-up, targeted flexibility work, and cool-down. The program progresses your range of motion gradually using resistance bands, a tennis ball, and bodyweight—all suitable for beginners.

**Weekly Commitment:** 7 days × 25 minutes ≈ 2.9 hours per week  
**Target Outcome:** Progressive improvement in shoulder and arm reach; ability to touch lower back by weeks 4–6 of consistent practice

---

## **Day 1: Shoulder Opener & Mobility Introduction**

**Warm-up (3 minutes)**
- Gentle arm circles: 20 forward, 20 backward
- Shoulder shrugs: 10 up-and-down
- Light torso rotation: 10 each side

**Main Session (18 minutes)**

1. **Doorway Pectoral Stretch**
   - Forearm on doorframe at shoulder height; step forward until stretch is felt
   - Hold: 30 seconds × 2 sets per side
   - Equipment: Doorway
   - Rest: 30 seconds between sets

2. **Shoulder Rolls (Active Mobility)**
   - Standing, roll shoulders backward 10 times, forward 10 times
   - 2 sets
   - Equipment: Bodyweight
   - Focus: Gentle, controlled motion

3. **Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch**
   - Bring arm across chest, gently pull toward opposite shoulder
   - Hold: 30 seconds × 2 sets per side
   - Equipment: Bodyweight
   - Rest: 20 seconds between

**Cool-down (4 minutes)**
- Gentle torso twists: 10 each side
- Slow shoulder rolls: 5 forward, 5 backward
- Deep breathing: 10 slow, full breaths

**Target Areas:** Chest, shoulders, rotational mobility  
**Equipment Needed:** Doorway, bodyweight

---

## **Day 2: Band-Assisted Shoulder Work & Release**

**Warm-up (3 minutes)**
- Arm circles: 15 forward, 15 backward
- Gentle shoulder shrugs: 10
- Wrist circles: 10 each direction

**Main Session (18 minutes)**

1. **Resistance Band Pull-Aparts**
   - Hold band with arms extended at chest height
   - Pull apart by spreading arms; squeeze shoulder blades together
   - 12–15 reps × 2 sets
   - Equipment: Resistance band
   - Rest: 30 seconds between sets
   - Intensity: Mild tension (not maximum resistance)

2. **Tennis Ball Shoulder Release**
   - Sit with back against wall, place ball between wall and shoulder blade
   - Apply gentle pressure; roll slightly up and down
   - 60 seconds per side (1 set each)
   - Equipment: Tennis ball, wall
   - Note: Relax into the pressure; no sharp pain

3. **Overhead Tricep Stretch**
   - Reach one arm overhead, bend elbow, gently pull with opposite hand
   - Hold: 30 seconds × 2 sets per side
   - Equipment: Bodyweight
   - Rest: 20 seconds between

**Cool-down (4 minutes)**
- Gentle arm swings: 10 each direction
- Passive shoulder stretch: Both arms across chest, 30 seconds
- Breathing: 10 slow breaths

**Target Areas:** Rear shoulders, upper back, triceps  
**Equipment Needed:** Resistance band, tennis ball

---

## **Day 3: Overhead Reach & Back-Touch Progression**

**Warm-up (3 minutes)**
- Arm circles: 20 forward, 20 backward
- Shoulder rolls: 5 forward, 5 backward
- Slow arm raises overhead: 5 repetitions

**Main Session (18 minutes)**

1. **Overhead Tricep Stretch (Progressive)**
   - Reach overhead, bend elbow, gently pull
   - Hold: 45 seconds × 2 sets per side (longer hold than Day 1)
   - Equipment: Bodyweight
   - Progression: Try to reach elbow lower toward opposite shoulder blade

2. **Wall-Assisted Back Reach (Direct Goal Practice)**
   - Face wall, reach arms up and back with palms flat
   - Gradually move hands toward back position
   - Hold: 20 seconds × 3 sets (measure hand position each time)
   - Equipment: Wall
   - Note: This directly practices your goal; expect gradual improvement
   - Progression: Hands move lower and further back each week

3. **Child's Pose with Rotation**
   - Kneeling, sink hips to heels, arms extend forward
   - Reach one arm underneath body, rotate gently
   - Hold: 45 seconds × 2 sets
   - Equipment: Mat or soft floor
   - Rest: 30 seconds between sides

**Cool-down (4 minutes)**
- Gentle forward fold: 30-second hold
- Supine shoulder stretch: Lying on back, arms across chest
- Recovery breathing: 10 deep breaths

**Target Areas:** Shoulders, triceps, upper back, arm reach  
**Equipment Needed:** Wall, bodyweight, mat (optional)  
**Key Focus:** This session directly practices your back-reach goal

---

## **Day 4: Myofascial Release & Rotational Mobility**

**Warm-up (3 minutes)**
- Arm circles: 15 forward, 15 backward
- Shoulder rolls: 10 total
- Wrist and finger circles: 10 each direction

**Main Session (18 minutes)**

1. **Tennis Ball Myofascial Release (Upper Back)**
   - Sit with back against wall, position ball on upper back muscle
   - Apply gentle, sustained pressure (not rolling)
   - 90 seconds total (move to different spots)
   - Equipment: Tennis ball, wall
   - Note: Feel for tender spots; gentle pressure is sufficient

2. **Band Rotational Shoulder Work**
   - Hold band with arms extended at chest height
   - Rotate torso side to side, keeping arms stable
   - 10 reps each side × 2 sets
   - Equipment: Resistance band (light resistance)

3. **Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch (Extended Hold)**
   - Bring arm across chest, gently pull
   - Hold: 45 seconds × 2 sets per side (increased duration)
   - Equipment: Bodyweight
   - Rest: 20 seconds

**Cool-down (4 minutes)**
- Slow torso twists: 5 each side
- Gentle arm swings: 10 each direction
- Meditative breathing: 10 slow breaths

**Target Areas:** Upper back, shoulders, rotational range  
**Equipment Needed:** Tennis ball, resistance band

---

## **Day 5: Strength & Flexibility Integration**

**Warm-up (3 minutes)**
- Arm circles: 20 forward, 20 backward
- Dynamic shoulder raises: 10 repetitions
- Gentle torso rotation: 10 each side

**Main Session (18 minutes)**

1. **Band-Assisted Back Reach (Direct Practice)**
   - Hold band vertically with one end in each hand
   - Lower hand behind back, upper hand pulls down from above
   - 8–10 controlled repetitions × 2 sets
   - Equipment: Resistance band
   - Note: Band provides support; hands move gradually closer together
   - Progression: Over weeks, narrow the hand spacing

2. **Supine Shoulder Stretch**
   - Lie on back, cross one arm over body at shoulder height
   - Gently pull arm toward opposite side
   - Hold: 45 seconds × 2 sets per side
   - Equipment: Mat or soft floor
   - Rest: 20 seconds between

3. **Band Pull-Aparts (Strengthening)**
   - Hold band with arms extended at chest height
   - Pull apart, squeeze shoulder blades
   - 15 reps × 2 sets (higher reps than earlier days)
   - Equipment: Resistance band
   - Rest: 30 seconds

**Cool-down (4 minutes)**
- Overhead reach and gentle sway: 30 seconds
- Cross-body shoulder stretch: 30 seconds per side
- Deep breathing: 10 breaths

**Target Areas:** Shoulders, triceps, rear delts, active flexibility  
**Equipment Needed:** Resistance band, mat (optional)  
**Key Focus:** This session combines strength and flexibility toward your goal

---

## **Day 6: Combined Release & Dynamic Stretching**

**Warm-up (3 minutes)**
- Slow arm circles: 20 forward, 20 backward
- Shoulder shrugs: 15 repetitions
- Light wrist rolls: 10 each direction

**Main Session (18 minutes)**

1. **Tennis Ball Release (Targeted)**
   - Position ball on shoulder muscle
   - Gentle, sustained pressure: 60 seconds
   - Equipment: Tennis ball
   - Note: Can do both sides in one session

2. **Scapular Wall Slides (Dynamic Strengthening)**
   - Back against wall, arms at goal-post position (90°)
   - Slide arms up wall, pause at top, slide down
   - 15 reps × 2 sets
   - Equipment: Wall
   - Progression: Pause longer at the top position

3. **Band-Assisted Flexibility (Extended)**
   - Hold band vertically, practice back-reach positioning
   - 10 controlled repetitions × 2 sets
   - Equipment: Resistance band
   - Focus: Smoothness and control; hands gradually closer together

**Cool-down (4 minutes)**
- Gentle forward fold: 30 seconds
- Supine shoulder stretch (both sides): 60 seconds total
- Recovery breathing: 10 deep breaths

**Target Areas:** Shoulders, upper back, scapular stability, arm reach  
**Equipment Needed:** Tennis ball, wall, resistance band

---

## **Day 7: Recovery & Maintenance**

**Warm-up (3 minutes)**
- Very gentle arm circles: 10 forward, 10 backward
- Shoulder rolls: 5 forward, 5 backward
- Soft torso rotation: 5 each side

**Main Session (18 minutes)** – *Light, recovery-focused*

1. **Child's Pose (Extended Recovery)**
   - Kneeling, hips to heels, arms forward, hold for 60 seconds
   - Equipment: Mat or soft floor
   - Purpose: Relaxation and gentle stretching

2. **Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch (Held)**
   - Bring arm across chest, very gentle pull
   - Hold: 60 seconds per side
   - Equipment: Bodyweight

3. **Gentle Myofascial Release (Light)**
   - Tennis ball: 30 seconds per shoulder (very light pressure)
   - Equipment: Tennis ball
   - Purpose: Maintenance, not aggressive release

4. **Overhead Reach (Recovery Position)**
   - Lie on back, arms extended, gentle stretch
   - Hold: 45 seconds
   - Equipment: Bodyweight

**Cool-down (4 minutes)** – *Extended recovery*
- Gentle breathing: 20 slow, deep breaths
- Mental focus: Reflect on the week's progress

**Target Areas:** Overall recovery, gentler stretching, reflection  
**Equipment Needed:** Tennis ball, mat (optional)  
**Recovery Note:** Day 7 is lighter to allow your body to consolidate gains. Full intensity resumes on Day 1 of the next week.

## Tips for Success

- **Consistency matters:** Daily practice is more important than intensity
- **Breathing:** Never hold your breath; breathe slowly throughout
- **Progression is gradual:** Expect noticeable change by week 3–4, significant gains by week 6–8
- **Recording progress:** Note hand position on wall each week (take a photo or mark position)
- **Recovery:** Day 7 is intentionally lighter; it helps your body adapt
